About Us

Bella's Coffee is named after my German Shepherd. She has been my partner in crime for the past 6 years and helped me through some of the toughest times in my life.

At Bella's Coffee, we are passionate about providing high-quality coffee and delicious food in a welcoming environment. We source our coffee beans from sustainable farms around the world and support local suppliers for our food offerings.
